# Support Channels & Policies

Welcome to the **Oistars Billings** support page. We are committed to maintaining a responsive, transparent support structure for business owners, developers, and reviewers.

---

## Getting Help

### 1. Documentation
Before opening an inquiry, please consult our comprehensive guides:
- [Official User Manual & Reviewer Guide](USER_MANUAL.md) - Step-by-step feature workflows and Play Store verification steps.
- [OpenSSF Best Practices Audit](OPENSSF_BEST_PRACTICES.md) - Detailed software supply chain and quality compliance report.
- [ProGuard & Security Architecture](PROGUARD_SECURITY.md) - Obfuscation and runtime hardening details.
- [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) - Development setup and contribution workflows.

### 2. Issue Tracker
For bug reports and technical issues:
- **Repository Issues**: Open a ticket on GitHub at `https://github.com/jpaul8282/Oistars-Billings2/issues`.
- Include device information, Android OS version, and steps to reproduce.

### 3. Direct Lead Maintainer Contact
For enterprise licensing, merchant settlement verification, or store review questions:
- **Lead Developer**: Jurgen Paul Westerveld
- **Email**: `westerveldjp@gmail.com`
- **Location**: Amsterdam, Netherlands (CET / UTC+1)
- **Response SLA**: Inquiries typically answered within 24 to 48 hours.

## Security Inquiries
For security vulnerability reports or sensitive financial data inquiries, do **NOT** open a public ticket. Follow the private disclosure protocol in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or email **`security@oistars.nl`**.
